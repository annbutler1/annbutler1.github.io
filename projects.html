About Me

Ann Capone Butler

Designer & Ceramic Artist

The inspiration for this series made for the Bronte Beach Collective was inspired by Bronte Beach itself, my favorite beach along the Sydney coast. I created this while studying abroad in Sydney, Australia and during my time there I spent many days at this beach. Some of the images encapsulated in this project are my own photography. This series encapsulates the calm and serenity of the beach, as well as the vibrant community that surrounds it.
